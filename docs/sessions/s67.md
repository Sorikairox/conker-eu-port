## Lessons
- **Header/Header2 arguments of `func_151602C0` are separate typed locals** (`Header`, `Header2` in structs.h), not fields of the spawner struct. m2c merges them into the stack struct `sp`, and spstruct/spsort cannot see that.
- **The "delayed float store" in the spawner prologue** (ref `lwc1 f16; swc1 f16; sb; jal; sb`, mine fills the lwc1 delay with the `sb`s) is a basic-block/scheduling-region effect. The permuter's `if ((a && a) && a) {}` (twice) reproduces it. Simpler barriers do not. The real construct is unknown (an inline function? a macro with a conditional?).
- Screening rate: `tryall` with generated permutations (`order_gen_example.py`: all orders of statement groups subject to data dependencies) reaches DIFF3-level drafts in one call. The last 1-3 instructions came from a permuter round or from a reasoned single change.
