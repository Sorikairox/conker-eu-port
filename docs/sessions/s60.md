3. **A named local that is only assigned from a constant (`f32 two = 2.0f`) costs a slot** (`func_150FEB84`: removing it fixed the frame but IDO then folds `x * 2.0f` to `add.s`, the ref keeps `lui at,0x4000; mtc1; mul.s`).
