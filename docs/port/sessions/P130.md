## Hand-asm notes (func_150A6070 / func_150A6324 / func_150A661C)
- Readable C, not asm2c. Projection: u = ((x m0 + m12) + y m4) + z m8 etc. in this association; outcodes 0x10 when
  w <= D_800D3AA8 (far clip), 0x20 when w >= D_800D3AAC (near clip), u: 8 / 2 when !(|u| <= -w) by the sign of u
  *before* the D_800D3AA0[0] scale, v: 1 / 4 by the sign of v *after* the scale (as the asm). Back faces:
  (v0 - v2)(u0 - u1) <= (u0 - u2)(v0 - v1) is dropped (NaN keeps the triangle).
- A triangle whose end reaches `end` is written but not counted (the asm returns the old pointer).
- The bit walk: after a non-zero word the index is rounded with `& 0xFFE0` (as the asm); a zero word adds 32.
- func_150A6324 loops are do-while (>= 1 id, >= 1 triangle per object), as the asm.
- Harness: `nmasm_shadow.py` extends the nmasm_vis5858 interpreter with add / addi / mult / mflo (exec of a patched
  copy of its Ref class; tools/port/difftest not changed). NaN words in the output are canonicalised (the
  interpreter writes Python's 0x7FC00000, the VR4300 / Unicorn 0x7FBFFFFF).
