## 0. difftest job limiter (tools/port/difftest.sh)

P229 and P231 both had 3 difftest jobs at once (cap 2: Docker memory is shared with the browser slot). difftest.sh now
runs the in-container job under a small semaphore: slots are dirs `/tmp/difftest_slots/slotN` (inside the container,
so per container) holding the PID of the job's in-container bash; slots are taken and cleaned under `flock`. A slot
whose PID is dead inside the container is stale and removed. A job with no free slot waits (one `difftest.sh: all 2 job
slots busy` line on stderr, then `got ... after Ns`). Knobs: `DIFFTEST_SLOTS` (default 2, 0 = off), `DIFFTEST_SLOTDIR`,
`DIFFTEST_SLOT_MAXWAIT=S` (give up after S s: `ERROR difftest.sh: no job slot`, exit 3). difftest.py is untouched.

Test: `tools/port/difftest/slots_test.sh` (own slot dir): 5 / 5 ok - a third job waits while 2 slots are held, a
waiting job runs once a slot frees (holder = `docker exec -d ... sleep 8`), a dead-PID slot is cleaned and the job runs
at once, the slot is released afterwards, `DIFFTEST_SLOTS=0` bypasses. In use this session: three mutant jobs ran at
once and the limiter kept difftest at 2. Selftest: see checks.
