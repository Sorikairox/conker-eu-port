## Tools

- difftest `DIFFTEST_EXACT="NAME,..."` or `'*'`: known prototypes are exact (like libultra). Without it, a scratch
  a2 that the reference sets right before a 1-arg call (func_150054C4 before func_15124B18) counts as an argument.
- difftest `proto_arity` now ignores comments. Commented-out old drafts (`//     func_15124B18(temp_f12, ..., 4 args)`)
  were taken as the prototype; the last match won.
- `difftest.sh` forwards `DIFFTEST_EXACT`.
- Pinned-record knob pattern: `DIFFTEST_ARG=0=80470000` plus `DIFFTEST_POKE=8047xxxx=...` sets fields of the a0 struct
  (see `port_work/p30/c490_knobs.sh`).
