## 1. Why `cfix.fix_selfproto` never fired (p17c: 29 CERR)

Two causes:
- **Oscillation with implproto.** implproto (earlier in FIXES) hoists the definition's own prototype (`//HOIST void f(...)`).
  selfproto then dropped the draft's self-prototypes *before* collecting the draft's lines, so the TU line of that hoist
  no longer counted as "the draft's" and was taken as "the file's prototype": the "fix" only removed the hoist, implproto
  re-added it, and so on for 6 rounds. Fixed: the draft's lines are collected first.
- **Irreconcilable types.** Most conflicts are `void` definition vs an m2c-era `s32 f(...)` prototype *in the function's
  own source file* (or pointer vs int params). The retype rule refuses those. New fallback: when the conflicting prototype
  is a single plain line of the source file, emit `//DROP <that line>` (#ifndef NON_MATCHING) plus `//HOIST <definition
  prototype>`. The TU compiles every C caller of the file; protocheck covers the other files.

Re-run `p17d` (30 = the 29 CERR + 1 DIFFER, `--no-gen`): **8 EQUIV** (all lint-clean), 9 DIFFER, 1 UNSURE, 12 CERR.
With the 3 p17c EQUIVs: 11 inserted. The remaining self-redeclarations are mostly against `include/functions.h` (no DROP there).

## 3. CERR autofixes (p18b sample: 96 CERR)

Categories of the last error (p18b): 24 redeclaration of another symbol, 17 syntax error, 12 subscript of non-array,
9 `-` operand, 7 self-redeclaration, 7 deref of non-pointer. Three new fixes:
- **spframe left `s32 sp.u48; /* compiler-managed */`** (17 syntax errors): the `DECL` regex required `;` at end of line,
  so m2c declarations with a trailing comment were not removed but renamed. It now accepts a trailing `/* */` comment.
- **implicit `int f()` vs the file's later `s32 f(...)`** ("redeclaration of 'func_X'", "Incompatible function return
  type"; s32 is `long`): `fix_implicit_proto` now also hoists the file's (unique) prototype line of X when X has no C
  definition and the draft does not declare X itself (also memcpy).
- **`fix_redecl` ignored `//HOIST` declarations** (rostr's `extern u8 D_X[];` vs variables.h's `extern s32 D_X;`): it now
  drops those too.
