# P326 (port-web, 2026-10-27): gameplay options "Longer breath" and "Longer tail spin"

User request (item 6 of the ConkerBFDReloaded comparison): two optional gameplay options, off by default.

## What the original game does

### Air under water: swim_water_physics (game_eu_78230.c, was func_1504B0FC)
Runs every game frame while Conker is in water (called from the walk handler while `in_water` != 0).
- Air counter: u16 at record +0xB2 (struct127 `unkB2`). While the head height (+0x1A6, from the skeleton) is more
  than 50 below the water surface (+0x118), it grows by the frame step `D_800BE9E4` (measured: 1 per 50 Hz retrace).
  When the head is up it is reset to 0, with a gasp sound by duration (>= 0x2D1: 0x168, >= 0x5D3: 0x1A7).
- 0x5A1 (1441): warning sound 0x499, and the counter jumps by 100. That passes 0x605 (1541) at once: one health lost,
  counter -0x32, then one more health every 0x32 ticks until death (player_hit_reaction 3 / 4).
- "Float" water (state block +0x20 bit 0, or +0x21) keeps the counter at 0 and pushes Conker up. The level 0x29 river
  is such water (the game sets +0x20 bit 0 every frame there), so no air is used there.
- HUD: underwater_air_meter_update (game_BC510.c) draws level = counter / 720; underwater_bubbles_update uses
  counter / 240. Both read the same counter, so they follow any change of its rate.

### Tail spin: obj_jump_swim_glide_update (game_83300.c, was func_1505F298), section 5 "in the air"
- ctl +0x31 (`unk31` in struct126, ctl = record +0x31C) is the spin counter. A jump sets it to 1 or 2; A again in the
  air (more than 1 above the floor) starts the spin: animation 0xE9, sound 0x61E, xz speed x 0.3, gravity 1, counter 3.
- Each frame it grows by `D_800BE9A0` (measured: ~0.58 per retrace). < 0xD: rise (y_velocity 8); < 0x11: propeller
  speeds up; then the fall speed is capped at 8 (slow descent).
- The spin ends when A is released after 0x13, at 0x3E (62) even with A held, or on landing (counter cleared).

## Commits
1. `6b71c152` "Longer breath" (PORT_OPT_LONG_BREATH = 4, main.js `longbreath`): in `#ifdef PORT`, below 0x5A1 the
   counter grows by half the frame step (odd tick carried). Safe time 1441 -> 2882 ticks (~29 s -> ~58 s). Warning,
   damage rate, gasps and HUD unchanged (the meter just empties at half speed). Also adds the probe (below). Fixed the
   description comment of swim_water_physics (it started with another function's name).
2. `a048f01a` "Longer tail spin" (PORT_OPT_LONG_TAILSPIN = 5, main.js `longspin`): the A-held limit 0x3E becomes
   `GLIDE_HOLD_MAX` = option ? 0x79 : 0x3E under PORT, plain 0x3E for IDO. 0x79 = 3 + 2 * (0x3E - 3), double the time
   from spin start to forced drop (~100 -> ~205 retraces). 2x rather than 1.5x: 1.5x (one extra second) is hard to
   notice; the descent speed is unchanged, so the range stays limited.
